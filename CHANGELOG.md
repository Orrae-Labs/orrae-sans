# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-27

- Relicensed the complete family under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Added editable UFO source and reproducible Fontmake builds.
- Expanded coverage to the Google Fonts Latin Core character set.
- Added combining-mark positioning and platform-compatible OpenType metadata.
- Added Google Fonts FontBakery validation and npm/CDN package metadata.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-08-26

- Adopted the Orrae Sans family name.
- Established the initial O, R, A, E, L, B, and S geometry.
- Extended the system across A–Z, numerals, and essential punctuation.
- Added custom kerning and the `ss01` alternate A.
- Added deterministic TTF, OTF, and WOFF2 generation.
